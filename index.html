What's taught

Every major calligraphy tradition, explained simply

01

Western / Modern Calligraphy

Brush-pen basics, letterforms and flourishing for cards, envelopes and wall art.

02

Copperplate & Spencerian

Pointed-pen technique, pressure control and the classic looped script.

03

Gothic & Blackletter

Broad-edge nib work, historical hands, and building a personal Gothic style.

04

Chinese Brush Calligraphy (毛笔书法)

Ink grinding, brush grip, and the core stroke families in regular and running script.

05

Arabic Calligraphy

Introductory Naskh proportions, qalam handling and letter joining.

06

Modern Brush Lettering

Everyday brush-pen lettering for journaling, planners and hand-lettered quotes.

How the class works

Two videos a week. No classroom required.

Everything is taught over video, so you can learn from anywhere, at your own pace, with your own set of pens or brushes.

  1. Sunday — New Lesson. A fresh technique or script is introduced, with the tools and paper you'll need.
  2. Monday — Practice Session. A slower, follow-along video to drill the strokes from Sunday's lesson.
  3. Sign up once. Fill in the short form below and you'll be added to the class list for reminders and materials lists.
  4. Ask anytime. Use the assistant at the bottom of this page for quick questions on tools, styles or what to expect.
